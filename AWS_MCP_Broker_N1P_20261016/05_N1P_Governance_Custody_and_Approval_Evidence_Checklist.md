## 3. Actual human adoption procedure — later private action only

This is a procedure template, not an instruction to any AI to approve.

1. Custodian presents the exact final frozen revision and composition digest to
   the authenticated authorized human in the trusted private system, together
   with the per-component and per-clause decision map and residual limitations.
2. Identity/authority verifier checks actual role grants and the signer/account
   control. Verify required separate approvers in their proper domains. Do not
   substitute repository edit rights or self-declared Owner status for authority.
3. The human personally performs the actual scoped adoption operation; required
   Design/Security adopters perform their actual respective operations. Use
   independently attributable, audit-verifiable event records bound to exact
   revision/digest. Partial events name exactly what remains incomplete.
4. Obtain event ID, immutable event reference, actual approved-at UTC, actual
   effective-from UTC no earlier than that approval, signature/verification
   receipt and approved scope. Do not substitute intent dates or AI clock values.
5. Verify signed bytes, trusted key/account, event identity, effective authority,
   covered digests, prospective scope and later revocation/expiry independently.
   Record only permitted nonsecret references in a **newly hashed** record.
6. Only after full required adoption and inputs are complete, commission a
   different N2 reviewer. This does not authorize technical experiments or G0.

Formal revocation procedure must identify who may revoke/reduce immediately,
how effective status is observed and how a replacement revision is adopted.
Restoration/re-enable/expanded trust is a new high-risk change, not emergency
reduction. Never resurrect used approvals through backup/rollback.
